## Enumerations

### AnchorKind

A kind of identity cue bundled to ground an Entity's identity.

- **business_key** — A business key such as SKU or serial.
- **physical** — Tag read or appearance embedding.
- **spatial** — A position-plus-time cue.

### AspectKind

The three faces of an Entity (three-phase aspect model).

- **business** — BusinessAspect — the ledger face (SKU, lot, ownership, quantity).
- **cognitive** — CognitiveAspect — the inferred face (derived beliefs).
- **physical** — PhysicalAspect — the observed face (pose, appearance, tags).

### ClaimKind

The subject-kind of a Claim; selects its confidence-decay half-life.

- **custody** — Who currently holds custody.
- **ownership** — Who owns / is responsible for something.
- **pose** — Position + orientation.
- **position** — Where something is.
- **presence** — Whether something is present.
- **quantity** — A counted quantity.
- **state** — A qualitative state (e.g. damaged).
- **tag_read** — A tag/label read.

### ExceptionDisposition

The escalation path for an Exception (deviation from expectation).

- **human** — Escalate to a human.
- **redelegate** — Change the delegation.
- **replan** — Re-plan around the deviation.
- **retry** — Retry the action.

### Method

How a Claim was produced. Feeds belief mediation as a tie-breaker rank (config/registry.yaml:claims.method_rank).

- **direct_measurement** — A direct sensor measurement.
- **hearsay** — Reported by another party, unverified.
- **inference** — Derived by reasoning.
- **ledger_of_record** — The authoritative business system of record.
- **sensor_fusion** — Fusion of multiple sensors.

### NormModality

Deontic modality of a Norm.

- **obligation** — Must do.
- **permission** — May do.
- **prohibition** — Must not do.

### NormStrength

Whether a Norm is a hard constraint or a soft preference.

- **hard** — Inviolable (e.g. the privacy floor, safety separation).
- **soft** — A preference tradeable against others.

### RealizationKind

How an abstract Action is realized (realization polymorphism).

- **human** — HumanRealization — a person, treated as a first-class realization.
- **informational** — InformationalRealization — an API/business step.
- **physical** — PhysicalRealization — a robot skill.

### Realm

The world a Claim belongs to. All realms coexist in one graph and are diff-queryable; only `real` describes the actual world (PROJECT.md §8: Realm).

- **hypothetical** — A counterfactual or what-if state.
- **planned** — An intended future state asserted by a plan.
- **real** — The actual physical/business world.
- **simulated** — A simulation twin's world (e.g. the morning-standup twin, F3).

### ReversibilityClass

How undoable an Action is. Drives autonomy and approval requirements — irreversible actions require explicit approval (PROJECT.md §8: ReversibilityClass).

- **compensable** — Not directly undoable but a compensating action exists (saga).
- **irreversible** — Cannot be undone (e.g. disposal). Requires approval gate.
- **reversible** — Fully undoable at negligible cost.
